# Global Vehicle Sales by Major Country

## Use Cases

- **Global market analysis**: compare sales scale and market share by country, brand, group, vehicle class, and month.
- **Product and model planning**: identify demand trends for EVs, fuel vehicles, and various classes across regions to support product layout.
- **Competitive intelligence and brand assessment**: analyze regional performance of brands, OEM groups, and specific models to assist competitor monitoring.
- **Sales forecasting and inventory management**: forecast market demand using historical monthly sales to optimize capacity, inventory, and channel resource allocation.
- **Regional entry and marketing decisions**: assess target-country and segment opportunities to support market entry, pricing, and marketing deployment.
