# Special-purpose Vehicle Monthly Sales

## Use Cases

- **Segment monitoring**: observe special-purpose vehicle demand changes by month, region, and model.
- **Regional channel management**: compare sales performance across provinces and cities to support dealer network layout and target breakdown.
- **Competitor and product planning**: analyze brand, series, and powertrain market performance to assist model planning.
- **Inventory and business forecasting**: optimize production and sales arrangements in combination with internal order, delivery, and inventory data.
