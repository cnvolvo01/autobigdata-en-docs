# Power BI Custom Development

> Data analytics services for automotive clients | From Excel to AI-assisted decisions, one-stop

---

## Service Overview

We provide **Power BI + AI data analytics services** that turn data scattered across Excel, business systems, and databases into analytics dashboards that are **easy to read, drill down, export, and alert on**, and use AI to quickly locate problems and uncover opportunities.

**Core philosophy**: we do not overturn the client's existing Excel habits; instead we make data capabilities connect seamlessly and upgrade step by step.

---

## Six Service Capabilities

| # | Capability | What We Do | What the Client Gets |
|---|------|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Data Cleaning** | Multi-source ingestion (Excel/database/API/cloud), deduplication, missing/outlier handling, caliber standardization, automated pipelines | A clean, trustworthy, reusable data foundation |
| 2 | **Data Modeling** | Star schemas, dimensional modeling, a DAX measure system, relationship and permission design | Unified calibers; reports and AI share the same source and accuracy |
| 3 | **Data Analysis** | Multidimensional analysis, YoY/MoM, forecasting, attribution, anomaly detection | From "seeing data" to "finding answers" |
| 4 | **Visualization** | Cockpits/large screens/mobile, themed design, smart narratives | See the business at a glance |
| 5 | **Easy Export** | One-click PDF/PPT/Excel export, subscription distribution, embed and share | Reports reach decision-makers directly, no more manual copy-paste |
| 6 | **Excel Collaboration** | Direct Excel connection, Power Query/Pivot reuse, post-export processing, add-ins | Reuse familiar tools for a smooth transition |

## Service Process

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Requirements Interview] --> B[Data Ingestion & Cleaning]
    B --> C[Data Modeling]
    C --> D[Dashboards & AI Q&A]
    D --> E[Export & Collaboration]
    E --> F[Training & Iteration]
```

1. **Requirements interview**: clarify business questions and key metrics (sales, penetration, gross margin, inventory, etc.).
2. **Data ingestion & cleaning**: ingest from the client's existing Excel/systems/databases and normalize.
3. **Data modeling**: build star schemas and unified metric calibers.
4. **Dashboards & AI Q&A**: visual dashboards + natural-language Q&A + anomaly alerts.
5. **Export & collaboration**: one-click PDF/PPT/Excel export, integrated with the client's existing workflows.
6. **Training & iteration**: deliver usage documentation and iterate as the business changes.
