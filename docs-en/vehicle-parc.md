# Vehicle Parc

## Use Cases

- **Regional market size assessment**: compare vehicle parc and growth trends across provinces and cities to identify key markets.
- **After-sales and channel layout**: estimate repair, parts, store, and service network demand based on the parc.
- **Vehicle structure analysis**: study model, energy type, and age structure to inform product and service planning.
- **Industry and business forecasting**: combine with sales, scrappage, and insurance data to support market capacity and business forecasting.
