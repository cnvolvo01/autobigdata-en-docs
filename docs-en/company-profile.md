# Company Profile


## About Us


Beijing AutoData Consulting Co., Ltd. was founded in July 2015. AUTODATA, meaning "automotive data", is a professional automotive market data service provider. Shuimu Siyuan (Tianjin) Technology Co., Ltd., founded in January 2016, is its subsidiary.

Our core team members each have more than 10 years of automotive industry research experience. We have built a one-stop, authoritative data product system around the entire vehicle lifecycle, covering: vehicle basic data (VIN & model, MIIT announcement numbers, passenger vehicle model library); new car sales (weekly and monthly sales of passenger / commercial / special-purpose vehicles); auto market research (vehicle parc, secondary-channel wholesale price, additional purchase and replacement); global market (major-country vehicle sales, passenger and commercial vehicle exports); used cars (transaction volume and city flow, valuation, value retention rate); accident & claims and transfer condition; auto finance (Car 5 / Car 7 essentials, person-vehicle verification); auto insurance (insurance-type expiry and insurance region); and NEV three-electric systems (traction battery report, electrification and ADAS configuration databases).

We were an early adopter of Microsoft Power BI in the industry. Centered on data modeling, visualization, and AI analytics, we turn authoritative data into insights that quickly reveal problems and uncover opportunities, delivering first-hand market information services to automakers, insurance companies, auto finance, used car, and auto consulting clients.
